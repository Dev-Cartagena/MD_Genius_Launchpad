# Spanish style guide (es-419)

Launchpad's Spanish is **neutral Latin American**. The audience is young, talented professionals building startups, so we use **tú**: warm, respectful and professional. It is never slangy and never "medical". The brand voice rules are the same as in English: clear, calm and reassuring, with no hype and no emoji.

## Rules

- **Address:** tú, using imperatives in tú form: "Inicia sesión", "Revisa tu postulación", "Guarda los cambios".
- **Punctuation:**
  - No exclamation marks, opening or closing (no ¡…!).
  - Use opening question marks: "¿Olvidaste tu contraseña?".
- **Case:** sentence case for headings, buttons, tabs and labels ("Crear cohorte", not "Crear Cohorte"). Proper names keep their capitals.
- **Gender:** use neutral wording where you can, e.g. "Te damos la bienvenida" instead of "Bienvenido/a". Avoid "@", "x" and "e" as gender endings.
- **Regionalisms:** avoid them. Don't use vosotros, "ordenador", "móvil" or "vale".
- **Formats:**
  - Dates and numbers come from Intl with the `es-419` locale. Never hand-format them.
  - Time is 12-hour as Intl renders it.
- **Length:** keep strings short. Spanish runs 20–30% longer, and buttons, tabs and badges sit on one line.
- **Brand and product names stay as is:** MD Genius Launchpad, Launchpad, MDGeniusAI, AI Coach (see the glossary), Demo Day.
- **Words we keep in English** because Latin American founders use them: startup, pitch, pitch deck, MVP, demo day, one-pager, check-in, feedback, networking.
