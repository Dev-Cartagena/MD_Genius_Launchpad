# Video: The pitch and the deck
Duration: ~3:15 · Format: 16:9 1920×1080 · Presenter: Maya Ortiz — calm, mid-30s Latin American founder-turned-investor, navy blazer, neutral studio

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:20)
- Type: presenter
- On screen: Maya centered, medium shot. Lower third in Space Grotesk, White Smoke on Navy #082B42: "Week 8 · The pitch and the deck". A thin Mist #97B7C1 line draws under the title.
- Narration EN: A deck will not raise money on its own. But a confusing one can lose a round in the first minute. Investors see many pitches a week and decide quickly whether to look closer. Today we build a deck that makes that decision easy.
- Narración ES: Un pitch deck no levanta dinero por sí solo. Pero uno confuso puede hacerte perder una ronda en el primer minuto. Los inversionistas ven muchos pitches por semana y deciden rápido si vale la pena mirar más de cerca. Hoy construiremos un pitch deck que les facilite esa decisión.

## Scene 2 — What investors look for (0:20–0:45)
- Type: motion
- On screen: Three White Smoke tiles appear on Navy in sequence: "Formidable founders", "Promising market", "Evidence of success". Credit below in Mist: "Paul Graham, How to Convince Investors". A fourth Steel #3A607D tile slides in beneath, labeled "AI: more than a thin layer".
- Narration EN: Paul Graham's essay How to Convince Investors names three things investors want: formidable founders, a promising market and, usually, some evidence of success so far. AI founders carry an extra burden. Investors have seen many products on the same models, so you must show you are more than a thin layer anyone could copy.
- Narración ES: El ensayo de Paul Graham "How to Convince Investors" nombra tres cosas que buscan los inversionistas: fundadores formidables, un mercado prometedor y, normalmente, alguna evidencia de éxito hasta ahora. Los fundadores de IA tienen una carga adicional. Los inversionistas han visto muchos productos sobre los mismos modelos, así que debes mostrar que eres más que una capa delgada que cualquiera podría copiar.

## Scene 3 — The one-liner (0:45–1:05)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A vague sentence types in Steel, "An AI-native operating system for the trades", then is struck through. Below it, a clear sentence types in White Smoke: "We help mid-size HVAC contractors fix equipment on the first visit with a voice assistant for their technicians." Three words underline in Mist in turn: "who", "job", "result".
- Narration EN: Start with one sentence that says what you do and for whom. Skip words like platform and revolutionize. Name the user, the job and the result. Then test it on people outside your industry. If they cannot repeat it back, rewrite it.
- Narración ES: Empieza con una oración que diga qué haces y para quién. Evita palabras como plataforma y revolucionar. Nombra al usuario, la tarea y el resultado. Luego pruébala con personas ajenas a tu industria. Si no pueden repetirla, reescríbela.

## Scene 4 — Answer the obvious questions (1:05–1:35)
- Type: split
- On screen: Left half, Maya speaking. Right half, six short questions appear one by one in White Smoke on Steel: "What do you do?", "For whom?", "Why now?", "What progress?", "Why your team?", "What are you asking for?". Credit below in Mist: "Michael Seibel, How to Pitch Your Company".
- Narration EN: Michael Seibel's YC article How to Pitch Your Company reduces a pitch to a short set of plain questions in plain language. When you finish, an investor should be able to say what you do, who it is for, why now, what progress you have made, why your team, and what you are asking for. If any answer is fuzzy, your deck will be too.
- Narración ES: El artículo de Michael Seibel en YC, "How to Pitch Your Company", reduce un pitch a un conjunto breve de preguntas simples en lenguaje simple. Al terminar, un inversionista debería poder decir qué haces, para quién, por qué ahora, cuánto has avanzado, por qué tu equipo y qué estás pidiendo. Si alguna respuesta es difusa, tu pitch deck también lo será.

## Scene 5 — The classic structure (1:35–2:00)
- Type: motion
- On screen: Ten slide thumbnails in Steel line up in a row, labeled in Space Grotesk: "Title", "Problem", "Solution", "Why now", "Traction", "Market", "Model", "Competition", "Team", "Ask". A Mist arc draws above them, rising from "Problem" (labeled "tension") to "Traction" (labeled "proof") and landing on "Ask". A small appendix stack sits to the right, labeled "Detail lives here".
- Narration EN: Most strong seed decks follow a similar order. Title and one-liner, problem, solution, why now, traction, market, business model, competition, team and the ask. Ten to twelve slides, one idea each. The order tells a story: the problem creates tension, the solution resolves it, traction proves it is real. Detail goes in an appendix you use to answer questions.
- Narración ES: Los mejores decks semilla siguen un orden parecido. Título y frase de una línea, problema, solución, por qué ahora, tracción, mercado, modelo de negocio, competencia, equipo y petición. De diez a doce diapositivas, una idea en cada una. El orden cuenta una historia: el problema crea tensión, la solución la resuelve y la tracción prueba que es real. El detalle va en un anexo para responder preguntas.

## Scene 6 — Honest traction (2:00–2:25)
- Type: split
- On screen: Left half, Maya speaking. Right half, a line chart in Mist rises over ten labeled weeks. Beside it, two separate White Smoke counters: "Paying customers" and "Pilots", with a thin divider between them. A Steel note: "Date and define every number."
- Narration EN: At seed, traction is often the slide that matters most. Show trends over time, not single totals. Separate paying customers from pilots, and never count an unpaid pilot as a customer. For AI products, add quality and cost: accuracy on your eval set and gross margin after inference. Investors will check in diligence, so anything you stretch now costs you trust later.
- Narración ES: En la etapa semilla, la tracción suele ser la diapositiva más importante. Muestra tendencias en el tiempo, no totales sueltos. Separa clientes de pago y pilotos, y nunca cuentes un piloto sin pago como cliente. En productos de IA, agrega calidad y costo: precisión en tu set de evaluación y margen bruto después de la inferencia. Los inversionistas lo verificarán en el due diligence, así que cualquier exageración te costará confianza.

## Scene 7 — The moat and the ask (2:25–2:45)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A question in White Smoke on Navy: "What stops the model provider from doing this?" Four Steel answers stack below: "Proprietary data", "Workflow embedding", "Distribution", "Evals on real tasks". Then a final card: "Ask: amount · instrument · milestone".
- Narration EN: Your competition slide must answer one question: what stops a funded competitor, or the model provider, from doing this? Point to proprietary data, workflow embedding, distribution and proof of quality on real tasks. Then end with a specific ask: the amount, the instrument and the milestone it reaches.
- Narración ES: Tu diapositiva de competencia debe responder una pregunta: ¿qué impide que un competidor con dinero, o el proveedor del modelo, haga esto? Apunta a datos propios, integración en el flujo de trabajo, distribución y pruebas de calidad en tareas reales. Luego termina con una petición específica: el monto, el instrumento y el hito que alcanza.
