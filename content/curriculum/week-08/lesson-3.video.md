# Video: Demo Day and beyond
Duration: ~3:00 · Format: 16:9 1920×1080 · Presenter: Maya Ortiz — calm, mid-30s Latin American founder-turned-investor, navy blazer, neutral studio

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:15)
- Type: presenter
- On screen: Maya centered, medium shot. Lower third in Space Grotesk, White Smoke on Navy #082B42: "Week 8 · Demo Day and beyond". A Mist #97B7C1 stopwatch icon in the corner reads "2:00".
- Narration EN: Demo Day is a starting gun, not a finish line. Two minutes on stage can open dozens of conversations. But the round closes in the weeks that follow, through meetings, follow-up and signed documents. Let's walk through all of it.
- Narración ES: El Demo Day es un disparo de salida, no una meta. Dos minutos en el escenario pueden abrir decenas de conversaciones. Pero la ronda se cierra en las semanas siguientes, con reuniones, seguimiento y documentos firmados. Veamos todo el recorrido.

## Scene 2 — What two minutes can do (0:15–0:40)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A horizontal progress bar fills over two minutes, divided into seven Steel #3A607D segments with labels and seconds: "What you do 15s", "Strongest number 15s", "Problem 20s", "How it works 20s", "Why you win 20s", "Market 15s", "Ask 10s". A White Smoke marker stops at "1:50" with the caption "Leave room for nerves".
- Narration EN: Two minutes is about two hundred fifty to three hundred spoken words. Enough for one clear idea and some evidence, not your whole deck. Paul Graham gives Demo Day two goals: explain what you are doing, and why users will want it. Open with your one-liner, then your strongest number, then a single customer story.
- Narración ES: Dos minutos son unas doscientas cincuenta a trescientas palabras habladas. Alcanza para una idea clara y algo de evidencia, no para todo tu pitch deck. Paul Graham le da al Demo Day dos objetivos: explicar qué estás haciendo y por qué los usuarios lo van a querer. Abre con tu frase de una línea, luego tu número más fuerte y después una sola historia de un cliente.

## Scene 3 — Rehearse until it is calm (0:40–1:00)
- Type: broll
- On screen: B-roll with a lower third in Space Grotesk: "Rehearse out loud. Time every run."
- Image prompt (if broll): Photorealistic documentary photo of a young woman founder rehearsing a short talk alone in an empty auditorium, standing on a low stage under soft house lights, a phone on a stand recording her, rows of empty seats in the foreground, calm concentrated expression, no logos or text in image.
- Narration EN: Write the script word for word and rehearse it aloud until you can deliver it calmly without reading. Cut until you finish at one minute fifty. Then practice in front of people who do not know your company. If they cannot repeat what you do, fix the script.
- Narración ES: Escribe el guion palabra por palabra y ensáyalo en voz alta hasta poder decirlo con calma sin leer. Recorta hasta terminar en un minuto cincuenta. Luego practica frente a personas que no conocen tu empresa. Si no pueden repetir qué haces, corrige el guion.

## Scene 4 — Run the process in parallel (1:00–1:25)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A Kanban board in Steel with columns "Target", "First meeting", "Partner meeting", "Committed", "Signed and wired". Many small White Smoke investor cards move in parallel across the first columns. A Mist caption: "Interest from other investors creates a deadline. Paul Graham, Investor Herd Dynamics".
- Narration EN: Treat fundraising like the sales process you ran in week seven. Build a list of forty to eighty investors at your stage and sector, and note who can lead. Then meet many of them in a short window. Paul Graham's essay on investor herd dynamics explains why: investors influence each other, and interest from others creates a deadline. A slow, one-at-a-time process loses momentum.
- Narración ES: Trata el levantamiento de capital como el proceso de ventas de la semana siete. Arma una lista de cuarenta a ochenta inversionistas de tu etapa y tu sector, y anota quién puede liderar. Luego reúnete con muchos en una ventana corta. El ensayo de Paul Graham sobre la dinámica de manada de los inversionistas explica por qué: se influyen entre sí, y el interés de otros impone un plazo. Un proceso lento, de uno en uno, pierde impulso.

## Scene 5 — Meetings and follow-up (1:25–1:50)
- Type: split
- On screen: Left half, Maya speaking. Right half, a checklist types in White Smoke: "Open with one-liner and strongest number", "Answer directly", "Ask about their process", "Follow up within 24 hours". A small Steel folder icon labeled "Light data room" slides in beneath.
- Narration EN: The goal of a first meeting is a clear next step. Open with your one-liner and strongest number, answer directly, and before you leave, ask how they decide and how long it takes. Follow up within a day. Keep a light data room ready, and share it when an investor is truly engaged.
- Narración ES: El objetivo de una primera reunión es un siguiente paso claro. Abre con tu frase de una línea y tu número más fuerte, responde de forma directa y, antes de irte, pregunta cómo deciden y cuánto tardan. Da seguimiento en menos de un día. Ten listo un data room liviano y compártelo cuando un inversionista esté realmente interesado.

## Scene 6 — Close committed money (1:50–2:15)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A speech bubble reading "We're in" in Mist turns into a signed document icon, then into a bank transfer arrow in White Smoke. Caption: "Verbal yes ≠ money in the bank". A second line: "Rolling close on SAFEs · documents reviewed by your lawyer".
- Narration EN: A verbal yes is not money in the bank. Paul Graham's advice is to close committed money: get documents signed and funds wired as soon as someone commits. With SAFEs you can close investors one at a time in a rolling close. Set a target and a deadline, share both honestly, and have your lawyer review everything.
- Narración ES: Un sí verbal no es dinero en el banco. El consejo de Paul Graham es cerrar el dinero comprometido: firmar los documentos y recibir la transferencia apenas alguien se compromete. Con SAFEs puedes cerrar con los inversionistas de uno en uno, en un cierre continuo. Define un monto objetivo y un plazo, comunícalos con honestidad y pide a tu abogado que revise todo.

## Scene 8 — Investor updates (2:35–2:50)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A simple email layout in White Smoke on Steel with four labeled sections that fill in: "Metrics", "Highlights", "Lowlights", "Asks". A Mist calendar strip beneath shows the same email repeating monthly.
- Narration EN: After the close, send a monthly update in the same format every time: key metrics, highlights, lowlights and specific asks. Share bad news early. It builds the trust you will need for the next round.
- Narración ES: Después del cierre, envía una actualización mensual con el mismo formato siempre: métricas clave, logros, problemas y peticiones específicas. Comparte las malas noticias temprano. Así construyes la confianza que necesitarás para la siguiente ronda.
