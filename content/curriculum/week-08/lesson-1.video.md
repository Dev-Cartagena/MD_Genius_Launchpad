## Scene 2 — Money buys a milestone (0:20–0:45)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A horizontal timeline in Mist. A Steel #3A607D bar labeled "Runway" extends from "Today" to a White Smoke flag labeled "Milestone". The bar shortens and the flag drifts out of reach with the caption "Too little"; the bar then extends far past the flag with the caption "Too much: extra dilution". - Narration EN: Fundraising is a tool, not a goal. Money buys time to reach the next milestone that makes your company more valuable. So start with the milestone, estimate what it costs to get there, and add a buffer. Common advice is eighteen to twenty-four months of runway, but your plan should set the number.
- Narración ES: Levantar capital es una herramienta, no una meta. El dinero compra tiempo para llegar al siguiente hito que hace más valiosa tu empresa. Empieza por el hito, estima cuánto cuesta llegar y agrega un margen. Un consejo común es tener de dieciocho a veinticuatro meses de runway, pero tu plan debe definir la cifra.

## Scene 3 — The early stages (0:45–1:10)
- Type: motion
- On screen: Three stacked Steel cards slide in from the right: "Pre-seed: team, insight, early evidence · angels, accelerators, small funds", "Seed: product and early traction · seed funds and angels", "Series A: repeatable growth · priced round, venture firm leads". A Mist note below: "Sizes vary widely by country, sector and cycle (as of 2026)".
- Narration EN: Early rounds have loose names. Pre-seed is often the first outside money, raised on a team, an insight and early evidence. Seed comes once there is a product and early traction. Series A usually follows proof of repeatable growth. As a general norm, many founders aim to sell around ten to twenty-five percent in each early round, but sizes vary widely.
- Narración ES: Los nombres de las rondas tempranas son flexibles. El pre-seed suele ser el primer dinero externo, levantado sobre un equipo, una idea clave y evidencia temprana. La semilla llega cuando ya hay producto y tracción inicial. La Serie A suele venir después de demostrar crecimiento repetible. Como norma general, muchos fundadores buscan vender entre diez y veinticinco por ciento en cada ronda temprana, pero los tamaños varían mucho.

## Scene 4 — The post-money SAFE (1:10–1:45)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A document icon labeled "SAFE · Y Combinator, 2013 · post-money version 2018". Three tags attach to it in sequence: "Valuation cap", "Discount", "Uncapped MFN", plus an optional fourth tag in Mist: "Pro rata side letter". Then a formula types in large Space Grotesk, White Smoke on Navy: "Cap SAFE at its cap: ownership = investment ÷ post-money cap". Example below: "$500,000 ÷ $10,000,000 = 5%".
- Narration EN: Y Combinator created the SAFE in 2013 and moved to the post-money version in 2018. A SAFE is not equity yet and not debt. The investor pays now and receives shares later, usually at a priced round. There is no interest and no maturity date. For a cap SAFE that converts at its cap, the post-money version makes ownership simple: investment divided by the post-money cap. Five hundred thousand dollars at a ten million dollar cap is five percent.
- Narración ES: Y Combinator creó el SAFE en 2013 y pasó a la versión post-money en 2018. Un SAFE todavía no es capital ni es deuda. El inversionista paga ahora y recibe acciones después, normalmente en una ronda con precio. No tiene intereses ni fecha de vencimiento. En un SAFE con tope que convierte a su tope, la versión post-money simplifica la propiedad: la inversión dividida por el tope post-money. Quinientos mil dólares con un tope de diez millones equivalen al cinco por ciento.

## Scene 5 — Dilution, step by step (1:45–2:15)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A horizontal stacked bar representing 100% of the company. Start: "Founders 90%" in Steel, "Pool 10%" in Mist. Step 1: two White Smoke segments slide in, "Angel SAFE 5%" and "Fund SAFE 10%", and the founders segment shrinks to "76.5%", the pool to "8.5%". Step 2: a new segment "Seed investors 20%" enters and the pool resizes to "10%". Final labels: "Founders 58.5% · Angel SAFE 3.8% · Fund SAFE 7.7% · Pool 10% · Seed 20%". Caption: "Simplified example. Use a cap table tool and a lawyer."
- Narration EN: Here is a simplified example. Founders own ninety percent and an option pool holds ten. Two SAFEs sell five and ten percent, so the founders drop to seventy-six and a half. Later, a seed round sells twenty percent and tops the pool back up to ten percent. That pool increase dilutes the SAFE holders and the founders, not the new investors. The founders end at fifty-eight and a half percent, still in control.
- Narración ES: Veamos un ejemplo simplificado. Los fundadores tienen el noventa por ciento y un pool de opciones el diez. Dos SAFEs venden el cinco y el diez por ciento, así que los fundadores bajan a setenta y seis y medio. Después, una ronda semilla vende el veinte por ciento y vuelve a llevar el pool al diez por ciento. Ese aumento del pool diluye a los tenedores de SAFEs y a los fundadores, no a los nuevos inversionistas. Los fundadores terminan con cincuenta y ocho y medio por ciento, y siguen al control.

## Scene 6 — Run a tight process (2:15–2:35)
- Type: split
- On screen: Left half, Maya speaking. Right half, a grid of small investor cards in Steel lights up several at once, labeled "In parallel". A Mist line at the bottom: "Breadth-first, weighted by expected value. Paul Graham, How to Raise Money".
- Narration EN: When you raise, Paul Graham advises breadth-first search weighted by expected value. Talk to many investors at once, focus on the likeliest, and close committed money as you get it. Every SAFE you add dilutes the founders, so keep a running cap table.
- Narración ES: Cuando levantes capital, Paul Graham aconseja una búsqueda en amplitud ponderada por valor esperado. Habla con muchos inversionistas a la vez, concéntrate en los más probables y cierra el dinero comprometido a medida que llega. Cada SAFE que sumas diluye a los fundadores, así que mantén tu cap table al día.
