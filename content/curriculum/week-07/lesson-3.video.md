Duration: ~3:15 · Format: 16:9 1920×1080 · Presenter: Maya Ortiz — calm, mid-30s Latin American founder-turned-investor, navy blazer, neutral studio

- Type: presenter
- On screen: Maya centered, medium shot. Lower third in Space Grotesk, White Smoke on Navy #082B42: "Week 7 · Distribution and growth loops". A thin Mist #97B7C1 line draws under the title.
- Narration EN: Many founders believe a great product spreads by itself. Sometimes it does. Usually it does not. In AI, where a competent team can build a similar product quickly, the company that reaches customers faster and more cheaply often wins. Today we look at how to find that path.
- Narración ES: Muchos fundadores creen que un gran producto se difunde solo. A veces pasa, pero es la excepción. En IA, donde un equipo competente puede construir un producto parecido en poco tiempo, suele ganar la empresa que llega a los clientes más rápido y más barato. Hoy veremos cómo encontrar ese camino.

## Scene 3 — The Bullseye framework (0:40–1:10)
- Type: motion
- On screen: Three concentric rings draw from the outside in. Outer ring in Mist labeled "What's possible: an idea for every channel", with small chips around it: "Sales", "Content", "SEO", "Ads", "Partners", "Events", "Community", "PR". Middle ring in Steel labeled "What's probable: 3 cheap tests". Inner ring in White Smoke labeled "What works: one focus channel". Three chips slide inward to the middle ring, then one moves to the center. Credit: "Weinberg and Mares, Traction".
- Narration EN: In their book Traction, Gabriel Weinberg and Justin Mares describe nineteen channels a startup can use to get customers. Founders tend to use only the ones they already know. The Bullseye framework fixes that. In the outer ring, write a concrete idea for every channel. In the middle ring, run cheap tests on the three most promising. In the inner ring, focus on the single channel that worked, until it stops improving.
- Narración ES: En su libro Traction, Gabriel Weinberg y Justin Mares describen diecinueve canales que una startup puede usar para conseguir clientes. Los fundadores suelen usar solo los que ya conocen. El marco Bullseye corrige eso. En el anillo exterior, escribe una idea concreta para cada canal. En el anillo medio, haz pruebas baratas de las tres más prometedoras. En el anillo interior, concéntrate en el canal que funcionó, hasta que deje de mejorar.

## Scene 4 — Test design (1:10–1:30)
- Type: split
- On screen: Left half, Maya speaking. Right half, a test card in White Smoke on Steel with four fields filling in: "Hypothesis", "Cost", "Time box: 1–2 weeks", "Success threshold". A final line underlines in Mist: "Track to revenue, not clicks."
- Narration EN: Write each test before you run it: the hypothesis, the cost, a time box of one or two weeks and the success threshold. Track results to customers and revenue, not clicks. A channel full of leads and empty of customers is a distraction.
- Narración ES: Escribe cada prueba antes de hacerla: la hipótesis, el costo, un plazo de una o dos semanas y el umbral de éxito. Rastrea los resultados hasta clientes e ingresos, no clics. Un canal lleno de prospectos y vacío de clientes es una distracción.

## Scene 5 — Funnels versus loops (1:30–2:05)
- Type: motion
- On screen: Left side, a vertical funnel in Steel with stages "Aware", "Sign up", "Activate", "Pay", and a dotted arrow pouring new users into the top. Right side, a circular loop in Mist with four nodes: "User gets value", "Shares", "Colleague arrives", "Becomes user". An arrow circulates continuously. Credit below: "Reforge, Growth Loops are the New Funnels".
- Narration EN: A funnel is a one-way flow. It shows where you lose people, but it has no memory, so every month you pour more in at the top. A growth loop feeds itself: the output of one cycle becomes the input of the next. Referral loops, content loops, paid loops and sales loops built on references all work this way. Measure a loop by how many new users each cycle produces per user, and how long a cycle takes.
- Narración ES: Un embudo es un flujo en una sola dirección. Muestra dónde pierdes gente, pero no tiene memoria, y cada mes tienes que volver a llenarlo. Un loop de crecimiento se alimenta solo: el resultado de un ciclo es la entrada del siguiente. Los loops de referidos, de contenido, pagados y de ventas basados en referencias funcionan así. Mide un loop por cuántos usuarios nuevos produce cada ciclo por usuario y por cuánto dura un ciclo.

## Scene 6 — Content and community (2:05–2:30)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A map-like grid in Steel with small Mist clusters labeled "Association", "User group", "Trade event", "Online forum". A White Smoke dot labeled "You" moves into the largest cluster instead of starting a new one. A second line types below: "Teach something useful. Anonymize customer data."
- Narration EN: For niche business buyers, content and community often compound best, because buyers trust peers more than ads. Do not build a community from scratch. Show up where your buyers already gather, such as associations, user groups and trade events, and teach something useful. If your content draws on customer data, aggregate and anonymize it, and make sure your contracts allow it.
- Narración ES: Con compradores empresariales de nicho, el contenido y la comunidad suelen acumularse mejor, porque confían más en sus pares que en los anuncios. No construyas una comunidad desde cero. Preséntate donde tus compradores ya se reúnen, como asociaciones, grupos de usuarios y ferias, y enseña algo útil. Si tu contenido usa datos de clientes, agrégalos y anonimízalos, y verifica que tus contratos lo permitan.

## Scene 8 — The technician referral loop (2:50–3:10)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A loop diagram in Mist with four nodes in White Smoke: "Tech solves a job", "Shares the fix", "Colleague's manager sees a demo", "New contractor's techs onboard". A Steel counter beside it reads "Referred demos per 100 active techs per month". A small shield icon labeled "No customer names or addresses" sits on the "Shares the fix" node.
- Narration EN: Their loop comes from technicians, who talk across companies and change employers often. A share-this-fix feature sends a colleague a read-only summary, with an invitation for their manager to see a demo. The founders track referred demos per hundred active technicians and keep customer names out of every shared summary.
- Narración ES: Su loop viene de los técnicos, que hablan entre empresas y cambian de empleador con frecuencia. La función "compartir esta solución" envía a un colega un resumen de solo lectura, con una invitación para que su gerente vea una demo. Los fundadores miden las demos referidas por cada cien técnicos activos y dejan fuera los nombres de clientes en cada resumen compartido.
