# Video: Choosing your go-to-market motion
Duration: ~3:30 · Format: 16:9 1920×1080 · Presenter: Maya Ortiz — calm, mid-30s Latin American founder-turned-investor, navy blazer, neutral studio

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:20)
- Type: presenter
- On screen: Maya centered, medium shot. Lower third in Space Grotesk, White Smoke on Navy #082B42: "Week 7 · Choosing your go-to-market motion". A thin Mist #97B7C1 line draws under the title from left to right.
- Narration EN: You have a product that some people want. Now comes a question that shapes everything else: how do customers find you, decide to buy and keep paying? That system is your go-to-market motion. Pick the wrong one and even a great product stalls.
- Narración ES: Tienes un producto que algunas personas quieren. Ahora viene una pregunta que define todo lo demás: ¿cómo te encuentran los clientes, cómo deciden comprar y por qué siguen pagando? Ese sistema es tu modelo de go-to-market. Si eliges mal, hasta un gran producto se estanca.

## Scene 2 — Three motions (0:20–0:50)
- Type: motion
- On screen: Three columns build in sequence on Navy, each a Steel #3A607D card with a Space Grotesk title: "Founder-led sales", "Product-led growth", "Enterprise". Under each, two short Mist lines appear: "Founders sell by hand / Learn what buyers value"; "Users self-serve / Value in minutes, low price"; "Large contracts / Long cycles, many stakeholders". A small White Smoke marker settles on the first column with the caption "Where most B2B startups start".
- Narration EN: Almost every early company starts from one of three motions. In founder-led sales, the founders find prospects, run calls and close deals themselves. In product-led growth, users sign up on their own, see value in minutes and pay with a card. In enterprise sales, large organizations buy through long cycles and many stakeholders. Nearly every B2B startup should begin with founder-led sales, even if it later becomes product-led.
- Narración ES: Casi toda empresa temprana parte de uno de tres modelos. En las ventas lideradas por fundadores, los fundadores encuentran prospectos, hacen llamadas y cierran tratos. En el crecimiento liderado por el producto, los usuarios se registran solos, ven valor en minutos y pagan con tarjeta. En las ventas enterprise, las grandes organizaciones compran con ciclos largos y muchas partes interesadas. Casi toda startup B2B debería empezar con ventas lideradas por fundadores, aunque después crezca con el producto.

## Scene 3 — Four questions (0:50–1:15)
- Type: split
- On screen: Left half, Maya speaking. Right half, a numbered list types in White Smoke on Steel: "1. Who decides?", "2. Contract value?", "3. Time to buy?", "4. Time to value?". After the fourth line, a caption fades in below in Mist: "Match the buyer, not the user."
- Narration EN: To choose, answer four questions. Who makes the buying decision? What is the likely annual contract value? How many steps sit between interested and signed? And how fast does a user see value? When the answers conflict, follow the buyer, not the user. Enthusiastic users who cannot sign are a channel into the account, not a sales motion.
- Narración ES: Para elegir, responde cuatro preguntas. ¿Quién toma la decisión de compra? ¿Cuál es el valor anual probable del contrato? ¿Cuántos pasos hay entre "me interesa" y "firmado"? ¿Y qué tan rápido ve valor el usuario? Cuando las respuestas no coinciden, sigue al comprador, no al usuario. Los usuarios entusiastas que no pueden firmar son un canal hacia la cuenta, no un modelo de ventas.

## Scene 4 — The AI free-tier trap (1:15–1:30)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A simple bar labeled "Free users" grows in Mist while a second bar labeled "Inference cost" grows faster in Steel. A thin White Smoke line labeled "Gross margin" slopes downward beneath them. Caption: "Check margin per free user first."
- Narration EN: A warning for AI founders. Product-led growth is tempting because demos are so impressive. But a free tier with real inference costs can burn cash fast. Check your gross margin per free user before you choose it.
- Narración ES: Una advertencia para fundadores de IA. El crecimiento liderado por el producto tienta porque las demos impresionan. Pero un plan gratuito con costos reales de inferencia puede quemar caja rápido. Revisa tu margen bruto por usuario gratuito antes de elegirlo.

## Scene 5 — Crossing the chasm (1:30–1:55)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A bell curve draws left to right in Mist, divided into five labeled segments: "Innovators", "Early adopters", "Early majority", "Late majority", "Laggards". A gap opens between "Early adopters" and "Early majority", labeled "The chasm" in White Smoke. Two captions float above the sides: "Buy a vision" on the left, "Buy a proven solution" on the right. Credit line at bottom: "Geoffrey Moore, Crossing the Chasm".
- Narration EN: Geoffrey Moore's book Crossing the Chasm explains why many startups stall. Early adopters buy a vision. They tolerate gaps and want an edge. The early majority are pragmatists. They want a proven solution and references from companies like theirs. Moore's advice is to win one narrow beachhead completely, so every buyer in that niche hears about you from a peer.
- Narración ES: El libro de Geoffrey Moore, Crossing the Chasm, explica por qué se estancan muchas startups. Los adoptantes tempranos compran una visión. Toleran vacíos y buscan ventaja. La mayoría temprana es pragmática: quiere una solución probada y referencias de empresas parecidas. Moore aconseja ganar por completo una cabeza de playa estrecha, para que cada comprador de ese nicho sepa de ti por un par.

## Scene 6 — The whole product (1:55–2:10)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A central Steel circle labeled "Core AI product". Around it, White Smoke tiles orbit in one by one: "Onboarding", "Integrations", "Support", "Error review", "Data handling", "Results report". The orbit settles into a ring labeled "Whole product".
- Narration EN: Pragmatists do not buy software. They buy an outcome. The whole product is everything they need to get it: onboarding, integrations, support, and for AI, a clear answer on errors, data and how to measure results.
- Narración ES: Los pragmáticos no compran software. Compran un resultado. El producto completo es todo lo que necesitan para lograrlo: incorporación, integraciones, soporte y, en IA, una respuesta clara sobre errores, datos y cómo medir resultados.

## Scene 7 — Motions change over time (2:10–2:40)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A horizontal path in Mist with three Steel waypoints that light up in turn: "Founder-led sales: first 10–20 customers", "Repeatable playbook: first sales hire", "Self-serve tier or enterprise team". A White Smoke flag at the first waypoint reads "Write your signal to change".
- Narration EN: Your first motion is not your last. A common path runs from founder-led sales for the first ten or twenty customers, to a written playbook that a first sales hire can run, and then to a self-serve tier for small accounts or an enterprise team for large ones. Decide in advance which signal tells you it is time to move, for example half of new customers arriving without a founder contacting them.
- Narración ES: Tu primer modelo no es el último. Un camino común va de las ventas lideradas por fundadores para los primeros diez o veinte clientes, a un proceso escrito que pueda ejecutar tu primer vendedor, y luego a un plan de autoservicio para cuentas pequeñas o un equipo enterprise para las grandes. Decide de antemano qué señal te indicará que es hora de avanzar, por ejemplo que la mitad de los clientes nuevos llegue sin que un fundador los contacte.

## Scene 8 — Fieldnote chooses (2:40–3:20)
- Type: broll
- On screen: B-roll with a lower third in Space Grotesk: "Fieldnote · founder-led sales to operations managers". A small label in Mist: "Fictional case. Founders' own data."
- Image prompt (if broll): Photorealistic documentary photo of a Latina woman in her early thirties in a work jacket talking with a middle-aged operations manager in a small HVAC contractor's office, a whiteboard with a job schedule behind them, service vans visible through the window, natural daylight, candid and calm, diverse people, no logos or text in image.
- Narration EN: Fieldnote's users are technicians, but the buyer is the operations manager at a mid-size HVAC contractor, and the owner signs. So Ana and Tomás chose founder-led sales, with Ana leading. Ana spent years as an HVAC operations lead, so she speaks the buyer's language about callbacks and the busy season. They rejected a free tier because every voice query carries an inference cost, and they set aside national chains, which would need security reviews and integrations, a whole product Fieldnote cannot deliver yet. Their signal to change: the first contractor who signs without ever meeting a founder.
- Narración ES: Los usuarios de Fieldnote son técnicos, pero quien compra es el gerente de operaciones de un contratista de HVAC mediano, y el dueño firma. Por eso Ana y Tomás eligieron ventas lideradas por fundadores, con Ana al frente. Ana fue líder de operaciones de HVAC durante años, así que habla el idioma del comprador sobre visitas repetidas y temporada alta. Descartaron un plan gratuito porque cada consulta de voz tiene un costo de inferencia, y dejaron para después a las cadenas nacionales, que exigirían revisiones de seguridad e integraciones, un producto completo que Fieldnote todavía no puede entregar. Su señal para cambiar: el primer contratista que firme sin conocer nunca a un fundador.
