# Video: Founder-led sales and your first ten customers
Duration: ~3:30 · Format: 16:9 1920×1080 · Presenter: Maya Ortiz — calm, mid-30s Latin American founder-turned-investor, navy blazer, neutral studio

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:20)
- Type: presenter
- On screen: Maya centered, medium shot. Lower third in Space Grotesk, White Smoke on Navy #082B42: "Week 7 · Founder-led sales and your first ten customers". A Mist #97B7C1 counter in the corner ticks from 0 to 10.
- Narration EN: Your first ten paying customers matter more than any ten you will sign later. They prove someone will pay, they teach you how buyers decide, and they become the references that sell the next hundred. They almost never arrive on their own. Founders bring them in.
- Narración ES: Tus primeros diez clientes de pago importan más que cualquier grupo de diez que firmes después. Prueban que alguien paga, te enseñan cómo deciden los compradores y se vuelven las referencias que venden a los siguientes cien. Casi nunca llegan solos. Los traen los fundadores.

## Scene 2 — A pipeline with exit criteria (0:20–0:50)
- Type: motion
- On screen: Six Steel #3A607D columns appear left to right, Kanban style, each with a Space Grotesk header: "Target", "Contacted", "Discovery", "Pilot proposed", "Pilot live", "Closed". Small White Smoke cards with fictional company initials slide from column to column. When a card crosses a column boundary, a one-line rule flashes in Mist: "Pain confirmed", "Scope, criteria and price sent", "Signed".
- Narration EN: Start with a list of fifty to one hundred named companies that match your ideal customer profile. Then give every deal a stage, and give every stage an exit rule, so you cannot fool yourself. A deal reaches discovery only when a call confirms the pain. It reaches pilot proposed only when scope, success criteria and price are in writing. Review the board every week as a team.
- Narración ES: Empieza con una lista de cincuenta a cien empresas con nombre que encajen con tu perfil de cliente ideal. Luego dale a cada trato una etapa, y a cada etapa una regla de salida, para no engañarte. Un trato llega a descubrimiento solo cuando una llamada confirma el dolor. Llega a piloto propuesto solo cuando el alcance, los criterios de éxito y el precio están por escrito. Revisa el tablero cada semana en equipo.

## Scene 3 — Work backward from the goal (0:50–1:20)
- Type: motion
- On screen: An inverted funnel builds from the bottom up in Mist and Steel. At the bottom, "10 customers". Above it, each layer appears with its rate in small White Smoke text: "÷ 1/2 pilots convert → 20 pilots", "÷ 1/3 calls become pilots → 60 calls", "÷ 1/5 contacts take a call → 300 contacts". A final caption: "10 weeks → 30 contacts and 6 calls a week". A small note: "Illustrative rates. Use your own."
- Narration EN: Now work backward. Say you want ten customers and half of your pilots convert. You need twenty pilots. If one in three discovery calls becomes a pilot, you need sixty calls. If one in five contacts takes a call, you need three hundred contacts. Over ten weeks, that is thirty contacts and six calls every week. Most founders discover they are doing a fraction of that.
- Narración ES: Ahora calcula hacia atrás. Supón que quieres diez clientes y que la mitad de tus pilotos se convierte. Necesitas veinte pilotos. Si una de cada tres llamadas de descubrimiento termina en piloto, necesitas sesenta llamadas. Si uno de cada cinco contactos acepta una llamada, necesitas trescientos contactos. En diez semanas, son treinta contactos y seis llamadas cada semana. La mayoría de los fundadores descubre que hace una fracción de eso.

## Scene 4 — Discovery is not a demo (1:20–1:50)
- Type: split
- On screen: Left half, Maya speaking. Right half, a five-step list types in White Smoke: "Agenda", "Current process", "Impact, in numbers", "Decision process", "Dated next step". Two role badges appear underneath in Steel: "Champion" and "Economic buyer", joined by a Mist line.
- Narration EN: A discovery call is not a demo. Set an agenda, ask how they handle the problem today, ask what it costs in numbers, and ask how they buy. End with a dated next step. Find two people: the champion who feels the pain, and the economic buyer who controls the budget. A champion with no path to the buyer gives you pilots that never close.
- Narración ES: Una llamada de descubrimiento no es una demo. Define una agenda, pregunta cómo resuelven hoy el problema, cuánto les cuesta en números y cómo compran. Termina con un siguiente paso con fecha. Encuentra a dos personas: el campeón, que siente el dolor, y el comprador económico, que controla el presupuesto. Un campeón sin camino hacia el comprador te da pilotos que nunca cierran.

## Scene 5 — Pilots that convert (1:50–2:15)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A one-page document builds in White Smoke on Steel, line by line: "Scope", "Duration: 4–8 weeks", "Success criteria + baseline", "Price", "Conversion terms". Two of the lines pulse in Mist. Caption: "No criteria = free trial. No conversion terms = start over."
- Narration EN: AI buyers often want a pilot on their own data. Write it down before it starts: the scope, a short fixed duration, two or three success criteria with a baseline, a price, and what happens if the criteria are met. A pilot without success criteria is a free trial. A pilot without conversion terms is a negotiation you will have to start again.
- Narración ES: Los compradores de IA suelen pedir un piloto con sus propios datos. Ponlo por escrito antes de empezar: el alcance, una duración corta y fija, dos o tres criterios de éxito con línea de base, un precio y qué pasa si se cumplen los criterios. Un piloto sin criterios de éxito es una prueba gratuita. Un piloto sin condiciones de conversión es una negociación que tendrás que empezar de nuevo.

## Scene 6 — Fieldnote's pilots (2:15–2:40)
- Type: broll
- On screen: B-roll with a lower third in Space Grotesk: "Fieldnote · 2 of 3 pilots converted". A small label in Mist: "Fictional case. Founders' own data."
- Image prompt (if broll): Photorealistic documentary photo of a young HVAC technician on a residential rooftop next to a condenser unit, speaking into a phone held near his face while checking a gauge, early morning natural light, candid, realistic workwear, no logos or text in image.
- Narration EN: Fieldnote started with three pilots. Ana proposed the same terms to all three: a twelve-month contract priced per technician, effective if first-time-fix rate improved and technicians used the product weekly. Two converted. The third did not, because the owner never met the founders and did not trust the numbers. Their lesson: meet the economic buyer before the pilot starts.
- Narración ES: Fieldnote empezó con tres pilotos. Ana propuso las mismas condiciones a los tres: un contrato de doce meses con precio por técnico, efectivo si mejoraba la tasa de resolución en la primera visita y si los técnicos usaban el producto cada semana. Dos se convirtieron. El tercero no, porque el dueño nunca conoció a los fundadores y no confió en los números. Su lección: conocer al comprador económico antes de empezar el piloto.

## Scene 7 — Closing and references (2:40–3:10)
- Type: motion
- On screen: A calendar strip in Mist with the last two weeks of a pilot highlighted in Steel and labeled "Review and propose". Then three White Smoke icons appear in a row with labels: "Reference call", "Short quote", "Peer introduction".
- Narration EN: Closing is mostly asking. Two weeks before a pilot ends, review results with both the champion and the buyer, and propose the contract. When a customer is happy, ask for a reference call, a short quote and an introduction to a peer. Draft each one for them. And when you lose a deal, write down why. Lost deals are some of your best product data.
- Narración ES: Cerrar consiste sobre todo en pedir. Dos semanas antes de que termine un piloto, revisa los resultados con el campeón y el comprador, y propón el contrato. Cuando un cliente esté contento, pídele una llamada de referencia, una cita breve y una presentación con un par. Déjale cada una redactada. Y cuando pierdas un trato, anota por qué. Los tratos perdidos son de tus mejores datos de producto.

## Scene 8 — Recap and exercise cue (3:10–3:25)
- Type: presenter
- On screen: Maya centered. Recap line in White Smoke on Navy: "Stages, numbers, discovery, pilots with terms." Below it in Mist: "Your turn: work backward to weekly outreach and draft one pilot proposal."
- Narration EN: Build the pipeline, do the math, run real discovery and put every pilot in writing. Now work backward from your own goal and draft one pilot proposal for your best prospect.
- Narración ES: Construye el pipeline, haz las cuentas, conduce un descubrimiento real y pon cada piloto por escrito. Ahora calcula hacia atrás desde tu propia meta y redacta una propuesta de piloto para tu mejor prospecto.
