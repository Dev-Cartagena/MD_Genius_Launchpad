## Goal

Turn this week's lessons into a go-to-market plan you can defend in one page, and a 30-day founder-led sales sprint you will actually run. By the end you should know who you sell to, how they buy, how many conversations you need each week and which channel you are testing.

Estimated time: 3 to 4 hours.

## Steps

1. Choose your motion. Answer the four questions from lesson 1 (who decides, contract value, time to buy, time to value) in one or two sentences each. Name your motion and the single buyer title you will sell to.
2. Define your beachhead and whole product. Describe your beachhead segment in one sentence. List every piece of the whole product a pragmatic buyer needs, and mark the pieces you do not have yet.
3. Build your pipeline. Create a spreadsheet or simple CRM with at least 50 named target companies, the person who feels the pain and the person who signs. Use the six stages from lesson 2 and write the exit rule for each.
4. Do the funnel math. Set a customer goal for the next 90 days. Using your real conversion rates, or estimates clearly labeled as guesses, work backward to weekly contacts, discovery calls and pilots.
5. Write a pilot template. Draft a one-page pilot proposal with scope, duration, two or three success criteria with baselines, price and conversion terms.
6. Run the Bullseye. Write one concrete idea for at least ten channels. Pick three, and write a test for each with hypothesis, cost, time box and success threshold.
7. Sketch one growth loop. Draw it as four or five steps in a circle and name the metric and cycle time you will track.
8. Plan the 30-day sprint. Week by week, list the number of contacts, calls and pilot proposals you commit to, which founder owns them, and the day each week you will review the pipeline together.

## Deliverable

A single document or slide set with:

- a one-page GTM plan: motion, buyer, beachhead, whole-product list, pilot template summary, three channel tests and one growth loop;
- a 30-day sales sprint table with weekly targets and owners;
- a link to or screenshot of your pipeline with at least 50 named targets.

Upload it to your roadmap task. Label every number as either your own data or an estimate.

## How it will be reviewed

Your mentor will check that:

- the motion follows from the buyer, contract value and buying process, not from what sounds scalable;
- the funnel math is internally consistent and the weekly targets match the 90-day goal;
- the pilot template includes measurable success criteria and conversion terms;
- each channel test has a written threshold and is cheap and quick enough to run within the sprint;
- the growth loop is a real loop, where the output of one cycle feeds the next, with a metric you can measure.

In week 8 you will report the first results of the sprint as traction in your deck.
